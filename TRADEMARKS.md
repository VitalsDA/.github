# Trademarks

VitalsDA™ and Vitals you can act on™ are trademarks of
[the rabbit hole tech, llc](https://therabbithole.com). They're unregistered (common-law)
trademarks, which is why you'll see ™ rather than ®; common-law rights apply from first use.

## What's fine, no permission needed

- Referring to VitalsDA by name in your own writing, talks, docs or code.
- Saying your product or integration "works with VitalsDA" or "integrates with VitalsDA".

## What needs written permission

- Using "VitalsDA" or "Vitals you can act on" (or anything confusingly close to either) in the
  name or tagline of your own product, service or company.
- Offering any hosted or SaaS service under the VitalsDA name.
- Reseller or partner use of either mark.

[VitalsDA/.github](https://github.com/VitalsDA/.github/issues).
